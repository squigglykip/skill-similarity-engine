Skills Analysis Methodology

Skills Analysis Calculation

Skills gaps are calculated by comparing the required skills for the target role against the skills in the current role, using our comprehensive skills taxonomy from Lightcast.

Transition Viability

Based on overall similarity score and skills overlap percentage. Higher scores indicate more viable transitions with existing skillsets.

Development Priority

Skills are prioritized based on their importance to the target role and transferability from existing capabilities.
