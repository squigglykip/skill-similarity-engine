Introduction

This document provides a practical guide for using and interpreting the outputs of the Skill Similarity Engine. While the technical overview explains how the engine works, this guide focuses on what you can do with the results and how to visualize them effectively for business purposes.

Whether you're a talent manager looking to develop career pathways, an HR business partner working on organizational restructuring, or a business leader planning workforce transitions, this guide will help you understand and apply the insights generated by the engine.
