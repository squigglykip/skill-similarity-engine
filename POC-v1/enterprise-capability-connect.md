## Speaking Notes

### Introduction (1 minute)
- Thank everyone and acknowledge their expertise in people development
- Frame the challenge: "We all know skills gaps are a critical issue, and we're exploring how we can support your work in addressing them"
- Position this work as a complement to their expertise: "We've been developing some tools that we hope will be valuable additions to your capability development work"

### Connecting to Our Learning Strategy (3 minutes)
- For each strategy point, first acknowledge their expertise:
  - "You're working on addressing skills gaps, and we're exploring how we can support that"
  - "Your learning initiatives are making a difference, and we're looking at how we can add to that impact"
  - "You're creating career mobility opportunities, and we're finding ways to make those pathways even clearer"
- Then show how this work can enhance their existing approaches
- Use simple analogies: "Think of this like having an additional lens to view development opportunities - it builds on your expertise rather than replacing it"

### What We've Discovered (2 minutes)
- Share human stories of insights, not technical details:
  - "We've been finding some interesting patterns, like how people in customer service roles often have skills that could support digital roles"
  - "We're seeing that leadership skills can be developed through multiple pathways, which aligns with your work in diverse development approaches"
- Emphasise the scale: "With over 35,000 colleagues in our organisation, we're using machine learning to analyse hundreds of millions of potential connections to find development opportunities that might otherwise remain hidden"
- Be concrete about outcomes: "This means we can work together to design learning journeys that build on what people already know"

### Moving Forward Together (1 minute)
- Emphasise partnership: "This work is designed to support your expertise, not replace it"
- Show how this complements their work: "We're looking forward to working with you to make development pathways even clearer"
- Invite collaboration: "We value your insights as we develop this further"

### Q&A Preparation (1 minute)
- Anticipate questions about how this supports their work:
  - "How can this complement our existing learning programmes?"
  - "How might this help us identify emerging skills needs?"
  - "How can we work together to integrate this with our current learning offerings?"
- Prepare simple, jargon-free answers that focus on partnership and support
