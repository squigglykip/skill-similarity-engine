Skill Similarity Engine: From POC to P&C Driven Initiative

Executive Summary

This document presents our Skill Similarity Engine project, which has successfully demonstrated the ability to analyse 15 million job relationships across global regions. The POC provided an understanding of the insights we will be able to gain from using a skills taxonomy, including potential talent mobility opportunities. We now propose a 6-month implementation plan to transform this POC into a P&C driven initiative that will enable data-driven workforce planning, career development, and strategic skill management.

The proposed implementation follows a three-phase approach, each delivering a complete capability:

  1. Core Similarity Engine (Months 1-2): Enhanced job similarity analysis with organisational-level performance
  2. Career Pathway Engine (Months 3-4): Career progression mapping and opportunity identification
  3. Skill Gap Analysis Engine (Months 5-6): Precise skill gap identification with learning recommendations

This solution will use HRIS and architectural data along with the Lightcast skills API to deliver a comprehensive talent intelligence ecosystem that significantly enhances our ability to develop, deploy, and retain talent strategically.

PART I: PROJECT IN REVIEW (PIR)

1. Executive Overview

Our Skill Similarity Engine POC successfully analysed 15 million job relationships between global regions, providing an understanding of potential talent mobility opportunities. This capability demonstrated significant strategic value at the PCEC meeting. The POC validated our approach and confirmed the business need for a comprehensive skill similarity solution using our core talent systems.

2. Key Business Impact Delivered

The POC supported strategic decision-making at the recent PCEC meeting, where we demonstrated potential connections between talent pools across global regions. This analysis indicated numerous career pathways across regions and highlighted skill alignment between different role types. These insights will help inform our cross-regional talent strategy.

3. Scale of Achievement

Starting with 4,000 individual job profiles across 18 departments, we performed 15 million job-to-job similarity comparisons—equivalent to comparing job roles across multiple global regions. This unprecedented scale enabled us to identify patterns that may not be visible through traditional analysis. When scaled to our full global workforce, the solution will process over 900 million comparisons, creating a comprehensive map of potential talent mobility across the entire organisation.

4. Strategic Alignment Confirmed

The POC confirmed alignment with three critical organisational initiatives:

  1. Talent & Global Workforce Strategy - Provides data-driven insights for cross-regional talent development and deployment
  2. Learning & Development Ecosystem - Creates skill gap insights to inform targeted learning recommendations
  3. People & Culture Systems - Uses HRIS and architectural data through intelligent skill analysis

The solution will work with our Lightcast skills API (currently in the 'front door' approval process), ensuring consistent skill taxonomy across all talent systems.

5. Critical Success Factors Identified

Our POC implementation revealed several critical factors that must be addressed in the P&C driven initiative:

Handling Global Workforce Scale

  • 900+ Million Relationship Analysis: The full implementation must reliably process our entire global workforce—900+ million job comparisons—while maintaining responsive analysis times. Our solution will build on POC innovations to ensure leaders can get answers when they need them, not hours or days later.
  • Faster Analysis for Business Decisions: By optimising system performance, we'll reduce analysis time from days to hours, allowing talent leaders to run multiple scenarios in a single day and make timely workforce planning decisions during critical business events.

Enhanced Skill Recognition

  • Improved Skill Matching Accuracy: The system will recognise complex skills like "Agile Project Management" as unified concepts rather than separate words, significantly improving match quality and leading to more relevant career recommendations for employees.
  • Contextual Job Understanding: By incorporating role context (seniority, leadership roles, business unit), the system will provide more meaningful career pathway suggestions that reflect real-world progression opportunities rather than just skill overlaps.

Intuitive Business Insights

  • Transparency in Recommendations: Business users will understand exactly why specific roles are recommended as career options, with clear visibility into which factors drove the recommendations, building trust and adoption.
  • What-If Analysis Capabilities: The system will allow HR leaders to simulate different career development scenarios (e.g., "What new roles could our Commercial Banking team transition to if we enhance their digital skills?"), supporting more strategic workforce planning.

10. Business Context Configuration: Adaptable to Different Business Problems

A key innovation from our POC is the ability to configure business context factors to adapt the analysis for different business objectives. The P&C driven initiative will offer a flexible configuration system that allows HR and talent professionals to adjust how various factors influence similarity calculations without requiring technical expertise.

Configurable Similarity Factors

The system will include configurable weights and thresholds for key business dimensions:

  • Career Progression Factors: Adjust how seniority levels affect similarity scores
    • Set higher similarity for same-level roles to encourage lateral mobility
    • Configure one-level-up promotion paths with appropriate similarity scores
    • Control penalties for multi-level jumps to reflect organisational promotion policies
    • Define how downward moves are treated (usually heavily penalised)
  • Role Track Considerations: Configure how transitions between individual contributor and leadership roles are evaluated
    • Adjust similarity between roles on the same track (IC-to-IC or leader-to-leader)
    • Set thresholds for IC-to-leadership transitions to support leadership development
    • Control how leadership-to-IC transitions are scored for technical career paths
  • Skill Type Weighting: Fine-tune how different skill categories impact similarity scores
    • Adjust importance of specialised skills vs common skills vs certifications
    • Configure how skill gaps in certain categories affect overall similarity
    • Set different thresholds for different skill types based on development difficulty
  • Location Factors: Enable or disable geographical considerations based on the business problem
    • Set to zero for fully remote work scenarios
    • Increase weight for roles requiring physical presence
    • Configure different thresholds for same-location vs different-location comparisons

Business Problem Adaptability

This configuration flexibility enables the system to address diverse business challenges:

Business Problem Configuration Approach Business Outcome
Critical Talent Retention • Higher weights for lateral moves
• Focus on specialised skills
• Lower penalties for track changes		 Identify immediate mobility options for high-value employees at risk of leaving
Organisational Restructure • Emphasise skill-based matches
• De-emphasise organisational hierarchy
• Reduced penalties for level changes		 Map displaced talent to new roles across restructured departments
Succession Planning • Focus on upward progression
• Higher weights for leadership track
• Moderate penalties for level jumps		 Build leadership pipelines with appropriate development pathways
Skill Transformation • Prioritise specific skill categories
• Reduce role track penalties
• Focus on lateral and slight upward moves		 Identify which current employees could transition to future-focused roles
Cross-Regional Talent Sharing • Disable or minimise location factors
• Balance skill categories equally
• Maintain level similarity requirements		 Identify talent mobility opportunities between regions/countries

Implementation Approach

This configuration capability will be implemented as:

  1. Non-Technical User Interface: Business-friendly configuration controls with clear explanations
  2. Configuration Profiles: Ability to save and name different configurations for specific business scenarios
  3. Real-Time Preview: Immediate feedback on how configuration changes affect sample recommendations
  4. Result Comparisons: Side-by-side comparison of results using different configuration profiles
  5. Contextual Documentation: Clear explanations of what each setting means for business outcomes

By providing this flexibility, the Skill Similarity Engine can quickly adapt to changing business priorities and support different talent strategies without requiring technical modifications.
